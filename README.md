### What I'm playing with lately

**[Meditation 31](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/meditation-31/id6791423097)** —
on the App Store. A wordless three-and-a-half-minute visual meditation: open it,
breathe, close it. Visuals drawn frame-by-frame in a SwiftUI `Canvas`, bell tones
synthesized at runtime with AVAudioEngine, and gradient direction driven by device
attitude so tilting the phone moves the image. Ships for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and
visionOS in 486 KB. No accounts, no analytics, no network calls at all.

**More generative work on iOS.** Tilt-driven particle flow fields, and the general
project of treating the phone's sensors as a brush rather than a controller.

**three.js and Processing.** A long-running habit more than a project — custom
geometry, textures, shader experiments. Most of it ends up on
[jkordy.art](https://jkordy.art).

**Unity, reluctantly.** An installation piece pushing Wolfram Language computation
into a Unity scene. Both halves are new to me, which is the point.

**Claude Code configuration.** An unreasonable amount of it — skills, hooks, and
agent setups, some of it in [ai-skills](https://github.com/jkordylewski/ai-skills).

*Twenty years of writing interfaces to other people's requirements, and the thing
I still find most interesting is what happens when nobody specifies the output.*
